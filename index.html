The Office of
Sam Solomon

Sam is an independent designer, art director, and creative consultant helping organizations tell their complex stories in approachable ways.


He helped create the Genome brand and magazine, helped redesign foreignaffairs.com and afar.com, and collaborated with Big Medium to help bring design systems to life for Cigna, Principal, and Ulta. His work has been recognized by the Society of Publication Designers, Communication Arts, Print, and Graphis, and he was selected to sit on the design jury for the SPD annual design competition.
